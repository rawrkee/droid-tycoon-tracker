Known History
Kyber Update
Kyber Rarity & Rebirth 36–40
  • Introduced the Kyber rarity, slotting in above Stellar
  • Kyber droids can glow Green, Blue, or Purple once activated — each color tracked separately in the Droidex
  • Rebirth 36–40 added across all 5 rebirth paths
  • Added the new Protocol-type droids SA-5, LOM, PZ, and TDA
  • Added the new Iconic droid D-0
  • Fusion droids now appear directly in the main Droidex alongside regular droids
  • Nova Crystal rewards per Rebirth level extended from RB 35 up to RB 40
August 15–16, 2026 — Stellar Event
Stellar Rarity & Rebirth 31–35
  • Introduced the Stellar rarity, slotting in above Galactic Mythic
  • Rebirth 31–35 became available at the start of the event
  • Stellar Astromech Missions and Stellar Droidex pages were added
  • Event modifiers: 2x Pickaxe Level, 2x Crafting Speed, and the usual 2x Credits multiplier
  • 2x chance of finding Stellar blueprints from the Sandcrawler during the event
  • Existing Galactic Mythic droids can be promoted straight to Stellar for 90,000 Upgrade Chips instead of waiting on the belt
August 1–2, 2026 — Chopper Event
New Iconic Droid: Chopper
  • Added the 8th Iconic droid, Chopper (Astromech), with the ability +50% Crit Chance & Damage
  • Chopper causes chaos at the Sandcrawler, dropping Green, Yellow, and Red crates around the map to break open for rewards
v1.21.0 — July 11, 2026
Base Barriers & Crafting Speed Rework
  • Base Barriers now block all projectiles
  • Crash Pad replaced with Crash Pad Jr.
  • Amban Sniper Rifle returned to the Weapon Wall
  • DJ-R3X companion bonus fixed
  • Beskar droids can now drop from Daily Quests and be upgraded at the "Lucky" droid
  • Mythic droids can now be upgraded at the "Lucky" droid
  • Worker crafting-speed companion perk doubled; SRB crafting-speed perk doubled per level (retroactive)
v1.20 — July 2, 2026
Mythic Timer & Rotating Hat Shop
  • Added the Mythic blueprint timer, spawning every hour at :55
  • R2-D2 Event Nova Crystal requirement reduced from 400 to 150
  • Completing a tower now rewards a burst of credits
  • Text chat now appears above players' heads in-world
  • Conveyor notifications recolored by rarity/variant, moved higher, larger font
  • Added a rotating Hat Shop for cosmetic droid hats, purchasable with Credits
Earlier 2026
Beskar & Galactic Rarities
  • Introduced the Beskar rarity tier, its own Sandcrawler spawns, and the Beskar blueprint timer
  • Introduced the Galactic rarity tier above Beskar, extending rebirth checklists to level 30
  • Flawless droids added — a rare cosmetic variant with a small chance on any craft
Tracker Updates
Site Features
  • Added Cloud Sync so progress can follow you across devices, or be shared with friends
  • Added the Droidex tab with a game-accurate layout, collection tracking, and a PDF export of missing droids
  • Added the Droid Reference tab with belt prices, sell values, crafting times, and upgrade costs for every droid
  • Added the Patch Notes tab
